What you can do in here

Erase, paint, fill, clone, history brush — with the brush ring showing the exact size of the mark before you make it, and the clone stamp previewing the patch it is about to lay down.

Select, lasso, magic wand — then move, resize, rotate, skew, delete to transparent, or fill. Alt+click with the wand takes a colour across the whole picture, which is how you cut a background away cleanly.

Paste a second picture on top and it lands as its own layer, sized to fit and never full-bleed, with the resize handles already on it: drag a corner to size it, drag the middle to move it, turn or slant it, then ✓ Apply to burn it in. Esc takes it away again and leaves no trace.

Straighten a crooked scan by tracing a line along an edge. Line and text to label. 🧻 Clean paper snaps a grey scan background to white without touching the drawing.

✨ Regenerate — type what you want changed and the AI redraws it. Select an area first and only that area can change. It is one undo step either way. (This is the only thing here that needs a network; if it is unavailable the button simply is not there.)

When you are done, ⬇️ Export lets you pick the format, the size and the quality — and shows the real file size before you save it — or copies the picture straight to your clipboard. Transparency is kept, so a cut-out stays cut out.

What each tool does, and every keyboard shortcut

Erase / Paint: drag over the image — click then Shift-click joins the two points with a straight line (Photoshop style), and Shift-drag locks the stroke to horizontal / vertical. Fill: click inside a shape to flood it with colour. Clone: click to set the source, then drag to stamp that area elsewhere (Alt+click re-sets the source) — once the source is set, the ring under your pointer shows the exact patch that will be stamped, so you can line it up before you press. History: brush the original picture back by hand. Select / Lasso / Wand: mark out an area, then fill it, move it, delete it, or use AI content-aware fill (Esc deselects). Delete (the key, or 🧽) cuts the selection away to transparent — so with the 🪄 wand you can Alt+click a colour to take it across the whole picture and delete every pixel of it in one go, which is how you get a clean transparent background. The ⬜/▨ Erase to button says whether Erase and the paint bucket cut through or paint white; in this app it starts on transparent. Move: drag inside a selection to cut it out and drop it anywhere (Alt to copy), or drag a text label to reposition it. Resize: drag the handles round the box — corners resize both ways, edges one way, and the corner opposite the one you drag stays exactly where it is. Drag inside the box to move it, hold Shift to swap between keeping the shape and stretching it freely, or type a width / height percentage in the panel. Rotate / Skew: turn or slant the selected object — or, with nothing selected, the whole picture, which is how you straighten a page that went into the scanner crooked. Drag on the image (Shift snaps to 15°) or use the sliders, and 📐 Straighten lets you trace a line along a crooked edge to level it in one go. Enter applies the turn, Esc cancels it. Line: drag from start to end. Text: click, type, then Enter.
📋 Paste a picture: press Ctrl+V (⌘V) — or the 📋 button — to drop whatever picture is on the clipboard straight onto this one. It lands as its own layer, scaled to fit inside the picture rather than covering it, with the resize handles already on it: drag a corner to size it, drag the middle to move it, turn or slant it if you want, then ✓ Apply to burn it in (Esc takes it away again and leaves no trace).
Scroll over the image to zoom (down to single pixels); hold Space (or the middle mouse button) and drag to pan; scroll over either slider to change it. Set the brush to 1 px for pixel-perfect edits.
Brush size: Erase, Paint, Clone, History and Line draw a ring around your pointer at the exact size of the mark they will make, at whatever zoom you are on. Change the size with the slider, the wheel over it, or [ and ]. With Clone the ring is filled with the pixels it is about to stamp.
✨ Regenerate: type what you want in the purple bar and the AI redraws it. Select an area first and only that area can change; with nothing selected the whole picture is redrawn. It is one step either way, so ↶ Undo puts the picture straight back if the AI missed.
